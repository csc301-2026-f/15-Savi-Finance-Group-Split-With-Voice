The Figma prototype is shared with view access so that the course staff can open and interact with it directly. If there are any issues, please email praneeth.suryadevara@mail.utoronto.ca

The interactive prototype demonstrates the main Group Split workflow, including navigating from the **group overview**, to **creating a transaction**, to **splitting that transaction**, and finally confirming the completed split. Note that the voice follow-up screen is in the first link but not the main flow.

Our partner contact, Ralph Maamari, suggested using Google Stitch to help generate and explore initial mockup designs. Savi provided the existing product context and prior starter work, while our team created, selected, and refined the Group Split interaction designs and assembled the final interactive flow in Figma.
